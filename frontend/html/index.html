How MediStock Powers Your Pharmacy

From supplier delivery receiving to patient checkout in three seamless steps.

1

Intake & Batch Registration

Receive supplier shipments and log batch numbers, manufacturing dates, expiry dates, and unit purchase costs into your active ledger.

✓ Real-time ledger intake
2

FEFO Auto-Dispense & Checkout

Add medicines at the counter. MediStock automatically picks the oldest active batch, applies GST, calculates change, and generates the bill.

✓ Oldest valid stock billed first
3

Live Monitoring & Reorders

Stock levels update instantly across all dashboards. Threshold alerts prompt timely supplier replenishment before medicines run dry.

✓ Zero stock-out surprises
