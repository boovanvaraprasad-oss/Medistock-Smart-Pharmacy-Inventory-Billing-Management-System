Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MediStock’s pharmacy operations, compliance, and billing system.

How does FEFO auto-allocation prevent medicine expiry and revenue loss? +

MediStock's First-Expiry, First-Out (FEFO) engine automatically prioritizes batches with the nearest expiry date during POS billing and prescription dispensing. This ensures older batches are dispensed first, preventing stock obsolescence, eliminating manual lot selection, and cutting medicine wastage to zero.

Can staff members have differentiated roles and access permissions? +

Yes. MediStock enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Pharmacists handle billing and returns; Inventory Executives manage batches and stock intake; Purchase Executives manage vendor orders; Store Managers review operations and audit sign-ins; and Compliance Officers inspect audit logs and narcotic registers. Only the Store Owner has administrative rights to manage user accounts, pricing, and master data.

How does MediStock handle low stock, out-of-stock, and expiry alerts? +

MediStock provides proactive 30-day expiry watchdogs and customizable minimum reorder thresholds. The system flags expiring batches, triggers high-priority alerts in the Notification Center, and displays actionable alerts directly on executive and pharmacist dashboards to prompt timely vendor returns or replenishment.

Is every stock adjustment and dispensation recorded in an audit trail? +

Absolutely. Every inventory intake, stock adjustment, return, and dispensed invoice generates an immutable audit record logging the exact timestamp, actor ID, action category, before/after values, and mandated clinical reasons. The audit trail is fully searchable and ready for state drug control inspections.

Can receipts and bills be customized with our Pharmacy GST and Drug License numbers? +

Yes. MediStock invoices seamlessly integrate your store's legal entity details, Drug License (DL) numbers (Form 20/21), GSTIN, contact numbers, and custom terms. Receipts can be printed directly on 2-inch or 3-inch thermal POS printers or downloaded as standardized PDF invoices with itemized batch numbers and expiry dates.

What hardware or operating systems are supported? +

MediStock runs seamlessly on any modern web browser across Windows, macOS, Linux, and tablets. It natively supports standard USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners, POS thermal receipt printers, and cash drawers without requiring proprietary drivers.
